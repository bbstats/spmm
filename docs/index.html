OpenRAPM

Player impact in points per 100 possessions, one rating per three-season block. Positive is good on both ends.

#Player Offense Defense Total Poss.
A multi-stage prior-informed RAPM: a role-and-age prior, then a gradient-boosted box-score prior, then a ridge on the on-court result, chosen by out-of-season prediction of held-out games. Source, method and every measurement: github.com/bbstats/openrapm. Architecture changes are welcome; the test that decides is scripts/45_holdout.py.
